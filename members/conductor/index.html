Conductor

Purpose

Section titled “Purpose”

Conductor is the rails. It is one front door for all off-session compute: it knows supply across subscription engines and the private-lane local pool, and it satisfies demand from any member. Members bring the what; conductor owns the when and the with-which-engine. It cannot refuse valid work — its reply is scheduling truth — so the requester keeps, modifies, or cancels against an ETA class rather than negotiating a gatekeeper.

Today

Section titled “Today”

As of 2026-08-19, conductor is live and load-bearing in generation 2. The request front door, brain enrolment, lanes, a decision queue, quota posture, daily progress snapshots, and per-member narratives are in place. Headless wakes run a bounded orchestrator cycle when invoked. The automatic runner is not installed, so wakes stay manual and builder still dispatches directly when needed. Maturity is 2 of 4 with a declared resting point (strategic session 2026-08-17): brain enrolment and dashboard IA v2 are treated as met; front-door completeness remains partial on later session-routing clauses; box residency waits on an act only the owner can take. Quota figures, account identifiers, and dispatch logs stay off this page.

Maturity against its own bar

Section titled “Maturity against its own bar”

Front door complete, brain enrolled, dashboard IA v2 complete, and box-resident.

Bar authority: ratified. Maturity: 2 of 4 — resting point declared 2026-08-17; leg 4 waits on the owner’s Phase C. Posture: resting-point.

A resting point is an honest pause, not abandonment. Headless work can still tidy and operate. Closing leg 4 — box residency after the owner’s Phase C — is an act only the owner can take. Until the runner is installed, unattended wakes are not a live claim even though the runner path is built and verified.

Boundaries and current edge

Section titled “Boundaries and current edge”

Conductor must not become a gatekeeper that rejects valid demand, must not publish quota headroom or account identifiers, and must not confuse a dry-run install path with an installed wake. It schedules; members author work. The honest edge as of 2026-08-19 is a resting point at 2 of 4: operative rails with manual wakes, and box residency waiting on the owner. Weaker word wins — a runway score never proves the uninstalled runner is live.

Story

Section titled “Story”

Seven days old when the vision was written. Early evidence already showed heavy use across lanes, including full build → review → fix → refute loops. The vision recorded that load rather than inventing a quieter future.

Conductor cannot reject work. That choice makes it a scheduler rather than a permission desk. Every request gets an ETA class; the requester keeps, modifies, or cancels. Quota is treated as a first-class citizen of admission — shape only on this page — so the ring can plan against included headroom instead of pretending supply is infinite.
