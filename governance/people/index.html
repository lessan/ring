People in the ring

Reality as of 19 August 2026 — This is the complete public role vocabulary, not a set of profiles.

The ring is personal, but this site is not a biography. It says what each role’s strand is for and leaves the person behind that role private.

Owner

Section titled “Owner”

The owner is the architect and ratifier. The ring extends memory and expertise, carries software work, and reduces the operational context the owner must hold; advice remains advice, and direction remains the owner’s.

Partner

Section titled “Partner”

The partner’s practical-assistant strand is for help with communication, calendars, and files through a personal interface. A separate protected strand exists outside that purpose and is intentionally not described on this public surface.

Daughter

Section titled “Daughter”

The daughter’s tutor strand is for voice-first help that can resolve what an assigned exercise means, explain it clearly, and keep the learner in charge of the interaction. Personal context belongs to her tenant, not to a public member description.

Younger son

Section titled “Younger son”

The younger son’s strands are a voice companion and a voice-led game-building path, with a physical-making project as another point of participation. The aim is agency and making, not surveillance or a public account of use.

Elder son

Section titled “Elder son”

The elder son’s current strands sit inside service and ventures: finding concrete ways to contribute, learn, and test possible projects. No dedicated member is claimed for him.

Household

Section titled “Household”

The household is the shared setting for storage and backups, local services, short links, the garden, making, service, ventures, and play. A household surface may be shared without turning the people using it into public subjects.

The tenant seam

Section titled “The tenant seam”

Personal and family facts live in per-person tenant records. Siblings may receive the bounded context needed for their purpose, but they do not copy the tenant into their own repositories.

Builder’s boundary is absolute: it does not read or write personal tenants. This is not a lower trust tier or a judgement made case by case. It is the structural seam that lets the executive system mind the ring without owning the people the ring serves.
